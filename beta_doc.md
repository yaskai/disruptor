### Beta Milestone Document

For beta, I set out to add variety and tension to gameplay as well as polish pre-existing features.    
Overall, I think development went well and I advanced significantly towards the goals I had set. 

Gameplay is now much more diverse in terms of player interaction with the addition of working AI navigation and gate/switch systems. 
It was very important to me for beta, that the player should not be able to complete a level simply by running straight towards exits  
without ever having to adjust their paths or strategies.

Lighting on environment geometry and character animations are a big improvement over alpha. 
There is still much to polish but, these additions were step forward.   

There are still a few elements missing from the game, most notably:

- sound effects and ambient/music tracks 
- level transitions 
- a main menu and/or title screen 
- proper save state reading and writing
- attack animations for Maintainer enemy
